About & Legal

ALWAYS Tracker is an independent, non-commercial, fan-made reference project created to track reported numbered parallels from the Harry Potter ALWAYS Trading Cards collection.

Unofficial project. ALWAYS Tracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with Panini, Warner Bros. Entertainment, or any other rights holder.

What the tracker records

The first version tracks the 100 Hogwarts Cards that may appear as Black Quartz 1/1 and Gold Quartz /10 parallels. For Gold Quartz cards, each serial number from 1/10 through 10/10 is tracked separately.

Reported status

“Reported” means that the tracker records a copy as having been reported. It does not constitute authentication, certification, proof of ownership, or a guarantee regarding the accuracy of any third-party information on which an update may have been based.

Intellectual property

All trademarks, names, characters, titles, and other intellectual property referenced on this website remain the property of their respective owners. References are used solely to identify the trading-card collection and individual cards tracked by this fan-made project.

Images

This first version does not reproduce official card artwork or promotional imagery. The site structure is prepared to display collector-provided photographs in the future if an appropriate publication process is introduced.
